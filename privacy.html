The DM's Perspective

Privacy policy

For the apps at thedmsperspective.github.io: the launcher, Bestiary, Character Sheet, Session Notes, Magic Items and Shops. Last updated 27 October 2026.

These apps help a Dungeon Master and their players run tabletop games. They run in your browser. There's no account to create with us, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing.

What the apps store, and where

  • On your device. What you enter (monsters, characters, notes, items, settings) is kept in your browser so the apps work offline.
  • In your own Google Drive, if you connect it. The apps save their data there so it syncs between your devices: in a private app folder only these apps can open, and, for character sheets, in a folder the apps create in your Drive. The apps can only see files they created themselves or files you choose to open with them. They can't see the rest of your Drive.
  • Shared with your table. A DM can share a character's sheet with that character's player through Google Drive, and the player sees it in their own copy of the Character Sheet app.

Google sign-in

Signing in with Google lets the apps read and write the files described above, and tells them your Google email address, which is used only to recognize your role at the table (for example, whether you're the DM). The apps ask Google only for access to files they created and to their private app folder.

To keep you signed in, your sign-in goes through a small service of ours on Cloudflare (the "key keeper"). It trades Google's one-time sign-in code for a long-term key, locks that key so only the service can use it, and gives the locked copy back to your device. The service doesn't store the key or your data. Signing out in the launcher tells Google to cancel the key. You can also remove the apps' access at any time in your Google Account, under Security, Third-party apps.

Live updates during a game

So that changes show up instantly on players' devices (hit points, turns, conditions, character sheets), the apps send that game information through Google Firebase. Each character's information is kept under its own long random key that only that character's player and the DM's devices know. It holds game details only, not your email or Drive files.

Who can see your data

You, and the people you choose to share with through Google Drive. We don't have a database of users and don't look at your files. Google, Cloudflare and Firebase process the data above only to provide these services, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Each app can erase its data on your device from its settings. Data in your Google Drive is yours to delete there. Live game information in Firebase is replaced as play goes on and holds nothing beyond the game.

Bug reports

If you use Report a bug in the launcher, what you write is emailed to The DM's Perspective along with your Google email address (so we can reply), which app it's about, and your device and browser type. Nothing from your characters, notes or campaigns is included. Reports are sent through Resend, an email service, and are used only to fix problems.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. For questions, use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for these apps.
